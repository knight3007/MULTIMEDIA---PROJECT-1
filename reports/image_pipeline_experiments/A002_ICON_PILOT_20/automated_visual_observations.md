# A-002 icon pilot — automated visual observations (AI preliminary)

Status: **AI desk observation, not a human decision.** The agent looked at A (current selected image) and B (SigLIP-selected icon candidate) at thumbnail size on 2026-11-22. These notes only point the owner at things to check in `review_gallery.html`. Human A/B labels: 0/20.

## Patterns worth checking

- **B is a pictogram where A missed the meaning.** rain (cloud with raindrops; A shows a lit-up city at night), swim (swimmer on waves; A is a static portrait), sleep (face with moon and Zz; A is a grid of bedroom objects), write (hand holding a pen; A is a slide titled "action plan"), remember (head with a bell), understand (head with a light bulb), drink (cocktail glass; A shows a row of juices).
- **B relies on English text inside the image.** today (calendar block labelled "TODAY"), easy ("EASY" sign), always ("ALWAYS" sticker), go ("GO!!" arrow), plus tomorrow and yesterday (almost the same circular-calendar icon, told apart only by the caption). In a game, printed English may give away the answer, and tomorrow and yesterday may be indistinguishable without their captions.
- **Watermarks or stock overlays in B.** rain, study, work and experience have a translucent "ICON-ICONS" box. always and different carry a depositphotos footer, and understand has a footer strip.
- **Weak or ambiguous B.** different (grid of app icons), because (abstract three-node graph), experience (gear, clock and person montage), important (exclamation mark: plausible but generic), difficult (person with puzzle pieces and lightning).
- **Suitability flag.** drink's B is a cocktail glass, which suggests alcohol. The owner should decide whether it suits learners.

## Not evidence of

- Superiority of icon search. No human review exists yet, the sample is 20 hand-picked hard items, and searches ran on a different date from the baseline.
- Rights or licensing. Many icon results come from stock or icon sites, so public use needs a separate rights check (META-001/D-003).
- Effect on simple concrete nouns. These were deliberately excluded.
