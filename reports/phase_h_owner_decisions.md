## Proposed decision record and image query selection

The review ledger is `data/vocabulary/semantic_decisions_v1.json`, keyed by persistent `vocab_id`, with `review_status`, approved `target_sense`, optional approved `image_search_intent`, reviewer, approval recording date, and rationale. Only the three `owner_approved` entries have runtime effect. The 20 `context` entries contain notes/written forms and remain `needs_decision`; a recommendation alone never approves a meaning.

At collection time for the canonical vocabulary path, an explicitly approved `image_search_intent` takes precedence and is passed unchanged to both DuckDuckGo and SigLIP. An approved sense without image intent stops that item's query selection. For other entries, the Qwen result remains the effective query. The generated manifest keeps the approved intent separate from the existing image metadata's original query and provenance.

The Qwen cache remains a model-output cache with its current signature and is untouched by overrides. `imagecollector.py` now selects an approved intent after Qwen preparation and before search/ranking, records query provenance for new metadata, and refuses to replace any existing JPEG or metadata when the effective query conflicts. Thus a fresh run for rows 4 or 9 reports an asset conflict until a separately planned replacement path is approved. The Qwen prompt, DDG search and SigLIP scoring are unchanged.
